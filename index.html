VR Brawler: Gesture Tester

Open this page in the Meta Quest Browser and press Enter VR. Wear your wrist straps and clear your space.

No buttons: grab the ground or walls and push to move, like Gorilla Tag · in mid-air, push a hand down hard for an air platform (3 per jump) · Trigger + punch = jab (either hand) · Grip + punch = power jab · Trigger + grip = block · A/X with arm straight out = set reach · B/Y = reset dummy

On a computer: 1 left jab 2 right jab · hold Shift for power jab · T changes tier · R resets dummy
